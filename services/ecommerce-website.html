THE PACKAGE

Conversion First, Aesthetics Second

A gorgeous storefront that doesn't sell is a portfolio piece, not a business. We start every e-commerce project by auditing the funnel, where people drop off between landing and checkout, and design specifically to close those gaps: clearer product photography layouts, friction-free checkout, and mobile-first flows since most of your traffic is on a phone.

Once the funnel works, we layer in the motion and visual polish that makes the brand feel premium. Conversion and craft aren't a tradeoff here, they're sequenced.

QUESTIONS

Common Questions About E-commerce Websites

Do you work with Shopify?

Yes, along with custom headless builds for stores that need more flexibility than themes allow. We'll recommend the right platform for your catalog size and growth plans in the discovery call.

Can you migrate my existing store?

Yes. We handle product, customer, and order data migration as part of the build, and test everything on a staging environment before the switch goes live so there's no downtime.

What about payment integration?

We integrate whichever payment providers you already use, or recommend one if you're starting fresh, Stripe and the major regional providers are all supported.

Do you keep optimizing after launch?

Optional. Since A/B testing infrastructure is set up during the build, many clients keep us on a light retainer afterward to run and interpret ongoing conversion tests.
