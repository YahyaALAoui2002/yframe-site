THE PACKAGE

A System, Not Just a Logo

A logo alone doesn't make a brand. We build the full system around it, color, typography, imagery direction, and tone of voice, so anyone on your team can create a social post, a slide deck, or a new page and have it still look unmistakably like you.

This is built digital-first. We design assuming your brand's main stage is a screen, not a business card, which changes decisions most traditional branding studios don't think about, like how a logo behaves at 32 pixels or how colors read on OLED displays.

QUESTIONS

Common Questions About Brand Identity

Do I need a full rebrand, or just a refresh?

Depends on what's actually broken. If your logo works but your site and social feel disconnected, a lighter system refresh is often enough, and cheaper. We'll tell you honestly in the discovery call.

Can this run alongside a website package?

Yes, and it's the most common way clients book it. We finalize the identity first, then carry it straight into the Business Website or Landing Page package so the site is built on a settled system, not a moving target.

Do you design print materials too?

Yes, as an optional add-on. Business cards, letterhead, and signage-ready files are available if your brand needs an offline presence.

What do I actually receive at the end?

Editable source files (Figma and vector logo files), a PDF guidelines document, and export-ready assets in every format you'll need, web, print, and social.
